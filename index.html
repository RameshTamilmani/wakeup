ABOUT SUBAM

Serving Homes Since 1991

Subam Bed House & Furnitures is a family-run showroom serving customers in Gobichettipalayam and surrounding areas.

We offer furniture, mattresses, pillows, bedding products, curtains, mosquito nets, carpets, mats and other home essentials.

We also provide customized solutions for beds, pillows, mosquito nets and curtains according to customer requirements.
